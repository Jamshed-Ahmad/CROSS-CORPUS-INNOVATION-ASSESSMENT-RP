1. OVERVIEW OF NEURAL ARCHITECTURES
================================================================================
The project architecture employs a staged, evidence-grounded pipeline:
  User Research Topic
          ↓
  Query Processing
          ↓
  OpenAlex API + EPO OPS API
          ↓
  Research Papers + Patents
          ↓
  Data Preprocessing
          ↓
  TF-IDF + BM25 + Sentence Transformer (MiniLM-L6-v2)
          ↓
  FAISS Dense Index
          ↓
  Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF, k=60)
          ↓
  Relevant Candidate Paper-Patent Pairs
          ↓
  Feature Engineering (10 Heterogeneous RAG Features)
          ↓
  Neural Relationship Models (Model 1, Model 2, Model 3)
          ↓
  Paper-Patent Relationship Score P(Related | Paper, Patent) in [0, 1]
          ↓
  Research Gap Candidate Formulation & Feature Extraction (12 Features)
          ↓
  Research Gap Detection Network (Multi-Task PyTorch Model)
          ↓
  Innovation Assessment & Recommendation

CRITICAL ARCHITECTURAL DISTINCTION:
  The 3 core deep-learning models (Model 1, Model 2, Model 3) are
  PAPER-PATENT RELATIONSHIP PREDICTION MODELS. They do NOT directly predict
  research gaps. Instead, they output a continuous relationship probability
  score P(Related | Paper, Patent) in [0, 1]. This score is subsequently consumed
  by downstream candidate gap feature extractors, the multi-task research gap
  network, and the multi-dimensional innovation scoring engine.

DIAGNOSTIC NOTE ON DUAL-TOWER CLASS DEFINITION:
  The project contains two dual-tower class definitions:
  1. `SiameseCrossAttentionANN` (in `backend/app/models/siamese_cross_attention_ann.py`):
     - Uses `proj_dim = 256`. Total parameters: 1,417,025.
     - MATCHES `models/relationship_model/relationship_model.pt` EXACTLY.
  2. `DualTowerCrossAttentionRelationshipModel` (in `backend/app/models/relationship_model.py`):
     - Uses default `hidden_dim = embed_dim = 384` for towers and cross-attention,
       producing 2,599,745 parameters (fusion_dim=3136).
     - Attempting to load `relationship_model.pt` directly into this class produces
       dimension mismatches (256 vs 384) and KeyError('state_dict') because the file
       was saved as a raw state_dict rather than a wrapped dictionary.
  Conclusion: `SiameseCrossAttentionANN` is the true source of truth architecture
  for the trained `relationship_model.pt` weights.

6. PRETRAINED COMPONENTS VS. CUSTOM TRAINABLE NEURAL COMPONENTS
================================================================================

To maintain scientific integrity and prevent conflating frozen foundation
models with research contributions, the system strictly distinguishes:

A. PRETRAINED COMPONENTS (External / Frozen Foundation Models):
  1. all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (`sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`):
     - 384-dimensional dense sentence embeddings from Hugging Face.
     - Role: Encodes raw academic abstracts and patent claims into dense vectors.
     - Parameters are FROZEN; NOT trained or modified during this project.
  2. ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2 (`cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`):
     - Pretrained cross-encoder reranker from Hugging Face.
     - Role: Second-stage query-document hard relevance filtering (0-100 score).
     - Parameters are FROZEN.
  3. FAISS (`IndexFlatIP`):
     - Dense maximum inner product search index.
     - Non-parametric vector index (no trainable neural weights).
  4. Okapi BM25 (`rank-bm25`):
     - Probabilistic sparse lexical retrieval model.
     - Statistical frequency counting (no neural parameters).
  5. TF-IDF (`scikit-learn`):
     - Statistical inverse document frequency matrix.
     - Non-parametric vocabulary weighting.
  6. Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF):
     - Algorithmic rank aggregation formula: sum(1 / (60 + rank_m)).
     - Non-parametric fusion function.

B. CUSTOM TRAINABLE DEEP-LEARNING COMPONENTS (Our Research Contributions):
  1. Model 1 Baseline ANN (`BaselinePaperPatentANN`):
     - Custom 5-layer MLP with dynamic Feature Attention (15,957 parameters).
     - Trained from scratch on cross-corpus labeled pairs.
  2. Model 1 Fine-Tuned ANN (`FineTunedPaperPatentANN`):
     - Custom MLP with LayerNorm, LeakyReLU, and residual attention (16,405 parameters).
     - Optimized via AdamW and learning rate decay on expanded dataset.
  3. Model 2 Deep Feature-Fusion ANN (`DeepFeatureFusionAttentionANN`):
     - Custom 4-branch modality projection + Group Saliency Attention (45,122 parameters).
     - Trained from scratch on multi-modal feature partitions.
  4. Model 3 Siamese Cross-Attention ANN (`SiameseCrossAttentionANN`):
     - Custom dual-tower paper & patent encoders + 4-head bidirectional cross-attention
       + multimodal fusion + classification head (1,417,025 parameters).
     - Trained end-to-end on multimodal text embeddings + RAG features.
  5. Downstream Gap Detection Network (`ResearchGapDetectionNetwork`):
     - Custom multi-task shared backbone with feature saliency attention and 3 heads
       (65,544 parameters).
     - Trained from scratch to detect, classify, and score evidence-grounded research gaps.

7. WORKFLOW INTEGRATION: RELATIONSHIP MODEL VS. RESEARCH GAP DETECTION
================================================================================

A critical architectural distinction is how the neural models interact with
the downstream innovation assessment pipeline:

Question: Does the neural ANN directly predict the research gap?
Answer:   NO.

The workflow operates in strict decoupled sequence:

1. Retrieval Phase:
   - User inputs a research idea/query.
   - Hybrid RAG (BM25 + TF-IDF + FAISS MiniLM -> RRF -> Cross-Encoder) retrieves
     top-k relevant research papers and patent prior art.

2. Candidate Pair Generation:
   - All retrieved paper-patent candidate pairs are constructed.
   - 10 heterogeneous RAG features (semantic, lexical, retrieval, metadata) are computed.

3. Neural Relationship Prediction:
   - MODEL 3 (Siamese Cross-Attention ANN) predicts the fine-grained relationship:
     P(Related | Paper, Patent) in [0.0, 1.0].
   - Model 1 and Model 2 serve as comparative baselines for this relationship task.

4. Feature Aggregation:
   - `extract_gap_candidate_features()` synthesizes gap indicators from:
     • Mean and minimum paper-patent relationship scores (from Step 3)
     • Extracted sentence-level limitations in papers and patents
     • Corpus-level coverage ratios (academic paper volume vs. patent volume)
     • Technology combination novelty and velocity metrics

5. Neural Research Gap Detection:
   - The 12-dimensional candidate vector is fed into `ResearchGapDetectionNetwork`.
   - The multi-task network predicts:
     • Gap Score: Likelihood of being an actionable technological bottleneck [0, 1]
     • Gap Type: Categorical classification into 1 of 6 gap families
     • Confidence: Evidence grounding confidence [0, 1]

6. Innovation Scoring & Validation:
   - `MultidimensionalScorer` combines gap scores, relationship probabilities,
     and passage evidence into a composite 6-factor Innovation Opportunity Score.
   - `EvidenceValidationService` filters out any gaps without strict cited passages.
